# Lost of Originality

**Lost of Originality** is a short, narrative-driven first-person exploration game developed for **GAMEATHON – TECHNEX 25–26**.  
The game challenges players to rediscover **independent thinking and originality** in a world guided by external voices.

---

##  Theme
**SHIFT**

The world constantly shifts between different states, and the player must learn to use these shifts thoughtfully to understand the environment and themselves.

---

##  High Concept
A first-person exploration game where players shift between dimensions to experience the same environment from different perspectives, encouraging observation, interpretation, and independent thought rather than objective-based progression.

---

## Core Idea
- No explicit objectives or instructions
- Learning through **exploration and observation**
- Player choices affect **understanding**, not the ending
- Narrative delivered through **environment, sound, and atmosphere**

---

##  Core Gameplay Loop
Explore  
→ Shift Dimensions  
→ Understand the Change  
→ Decide & Act  
→ Consequence & Insight  

---

##  SHIFT Mechanics
The game features **three dimension states**, each with a specific purpose:

- **Physical Shift**  
  Allows movement through normally inaccessible spaces

- **Interaction Shift**  
  Enables interaction with selected objects in the environment

- **Reveal Shift**  
  Reveals objects and elements lost in time

SHIFT usage is intentionally limited to promote thoughtful decision-making.

---

## Art & Atmosphere
- **Art Style:** Low Poly  
- **Visual Inspiration:** No Man’s Sky  
- **Environment Creation:** Blender  
- Focus on lighting, fog, and mood to support immersion

---

##  Sound Design
- Original Sound Track (OST)
- Dynamic audio that changes with player interaction and dimension shifts
- Sound is used as feedback instead of UI-heavy indicators

---

##  Technology Stack
- **Game Engine:** Unity  
- **Programming Language:** C#  
- **Tools:** Blender, Visual Studio Code, Affinity, PowerPoint  
- **Platform:** PC  

---

## Prototype Scope
- Single, small playable environment
- Core SHIFT mechanic implemented
- First-person movement and camera
- Minimal interaction system
- Focus on atmosphere, sound, and exploration
- Designed to be completed in one sitting
